Dassai (also called AlphaX) is an AI-native research workbench purpose-built for self-directed A-share investors. Instead of scattering your research across broker apps, financial news sites, and general-purpose AI tools, Dassai brings your holdings, watchlists, and evidence-backed analysis into a single traceable workspace. You ask questions in plain Chinese, receive answers grounded in verifiable sources, and save your conclusions as interactive Research Workbenches you can revisit and review over time.

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for Dassai">
    Create your account using WeChat OAuth or email. On first login, complete the quick activation flow: confirm your A-share holdings or watchlist, save your first Research Workbench, and record at least one Judgment Premise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first research question">
    Use the global Ask bar — present on every page — to type a natural-language question in Chinese. For example: **"茅台最新财报有哪些值得关注的变化？"** Dassai's Super Agent resolves your intent, fetches verifiable data, and returns a structured, source-cited answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save insights as a Workbench">
    Select one or more components from the AI answer — metric cards, trend charts, disclosure summaries, evidence facts — and save them as a named Research Workbench. Your Workbench stays live, updating as new data arrives and alerting you when evidence challenges your recorded Judgment Premises.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Dassai is a research tool only. It does not provide buy or sell recommendations, connect to brokerage accounts, execute trades, or offer personalized investment advice. All content is for research reference and may be delayed or incomplete. Nothing in Dassai constitutes investment advice.
</Note>
